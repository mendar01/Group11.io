## Citation format for an EAP presentation

**On the slide (short form):**
> Source: World Inequality Report 2022 (WID.world)

**Spoken (long form):**
> "According to the OECD's *Society at a Glance 2024*…"
> "The U.S. Federal Reserve's Survey of Consumer Finances shows that…"

**Written reference (Harvard style), if your tutor asks for a bibliography:**

> OECD (2024) *Society at a Glance 2024: OECD Social Indicators*. Paris: OECD Publishing. Available at: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/society-at-a-glance-2024_918d8db3-en.html
>
> World Inequality Lab (2021) *World Inequality Report 2022*. Paris: World Inequality Lab. Available at: https://wir2022.wid.world
>
> Oxfam International (2025) *Billionaire wealth surges by $2 trillion in 2024*. 20 January. Available at: https://www.oxfam.org/en/press-releases/billionaire-wealth-surges-2-trillion-2024

## A note on using sources critically

- Oxfam is an **advocacy organisation** — its figures are accurate and its methodology is published, but it argues a position. Say so if asked; this is exactly the kind of critical awareness EAP rewards.
- The World Inequality Report and the World Bank use **different methods** and can give different Gini values. When two sources disagree, cite both rather than picking the more dramatic number.
- Always give the **year of the data**, not just the year of the report. "2021 data, published 2021" is stronger than "recent data".
